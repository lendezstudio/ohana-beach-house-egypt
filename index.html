Welcome to Ohana

Meet You by the Sea

Some days are made for the beach. Others are for slowing down by the pool and enjoying the North Coast at your own pace.

Located on the North Coast of New Alamein City, Ohana Beach House is designed to give guests a luxurious and comfortable experience while taking in the beautiful scenery around them. Come for an easy beach day, a relaxed poolside escape, or one of Ohana’s social and event moments.

Good to Know

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Call the team or send an inquiry below.

Where is Ohana Beach House?

Ohana Beach House is on the North Coast of New Alamein City, Egypt. Contact the team for directions before your visit.

Does Ohana have a pool?

Yes. Ohana is a beach and pool house, so you can enjoy time on the beach and relaxed moments by the pool.

Does Ohana host events?

Yes. Ohana hosts social and music events by the coast. Upcoming dates are announced in the What’s On section and on Ohana’s Facebook page.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservation requirements may vary by date and event. Contact Ohana before your visit for current availability and entry details.

Can I host a private event at Ohana?

Private-event availability and services are confirmed directly with the Ohana team. Use the inquiry form to share your date, guest count and idea.

How do I contact Ohana?

Call +20 102 241 1190, message Ohana on Facebook, or send an inquiry using the form on this page.
